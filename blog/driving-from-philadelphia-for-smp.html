Driving from Philadelphia for SMP: what the day looks like

A lot of my clients live in Philly and the suburbs. The studio is about 31 miles from Philadelphia International Airport, and almost all of it is I-95 South.

Getting here

Take I-95 South to Exit 5A-B (DE-141 North), then Kirkwood Highway (DE-2) to Annand Drive. Google Maps puts it at about an hour at weekday rush hour. If you'd rather not drive, SEPTA's Wilmington/Newark Line stops at Churchmans Crossing, about 4 miles from the studio.

The consult

Free. We look at your hair loss, talk about what you want, and I draw out a hairline so you can see it before anything happens. If SMP isn't right for you, I'll tell you.

Session days

Each session runs about 2 to 4 hours. Most people need 3 to 4 sessions, 2 to 3 weeks apart. You can go back to work the same day. Just skip the gym and keep your scalp dry for 3 to 4 days.

Paying for it

Financing is available through PayPal Credit and Cherry. You can apply for Cherry online before you come in.
