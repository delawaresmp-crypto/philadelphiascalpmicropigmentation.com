SMP after a hair transplant: filling in what didn't grow

Plenty of my clients already had a transplant. Some loved it but wanted it to look thicker. Some were left with scars they can't stop thinking about.

Thin transplant results

Grafts only cover so much. SMP adds pigment between the hairs so the scalp doesn't show through, which makes the transplant look fuller.

FUE and FUT scars

Healed FUE dot scars and FUT strip scars can usually be softened with SMP so they blend in when you wear your hair short.

Timing

Let everything heal first. Send me photos and tell me when you had the procedure, and we'll figure out the right time to start.
