What SMP is

Tiny pigment dots that look like hair follicles

Scalp micropigmentation is a cosmetic tattoo for hair loss. I place thousands of small dots in the top layer of the scalp so it looks like a fresh buzz cut, or so thin spots stop showing through your hair.

No surgery. No pills. No hair system. You walk out and go back to work the same day.

  • Rebuild a receding or bald hairline
  • Add density where your hair is thinning
  • Fill in a hair transplant that came in thin
  • Hide FUE and FUT scars
  • Cover alopecia patches
  • Fix bad SMP from somewhere else

How it works

From first text to finished hairline

  1. Free consult. Come in or send photos. We talk about the hairline you want and what fits your face and age.
  2. Session one. I lay down the base and the hairline shape. Usually 2 to 4 hours.
  3. Sessions two and three. Spaced 2 to 3 weeks apart. This is where density gets built and the look gets dialed in.
  4. Healed and done. Most people are finished in about 5 to 8 weeks. Touch-ups every few years keep it sharp.
