What I can do for you

Every head is different. These are the main things people come to me for.

Hairline restoration

For a receding or fully bald hairline. I design a hairline that fits your face and your age, not a straight line that screams fake. Most guys go with a short buzz cut look.

Learn more

Density for thinning hair

You keep your hair longer and I add pigment between the hairs so the scalp stops shining through. You don't have to shave your head for this.

Learn more

SMP for women

Thinning along the part or crown. Done through existing hair so it blends in. Women often come in after trying everything else first.

Learn more

Hair transplant help

Transplant came in thin or left you with scars? SMP fills in the gaps between grafts and can hide healed FUE dots and FUT strip scars.

Learn more

Alopecia

Patches from alopecia can be blended in so they don't stand out. We talk about whether your alopecia is stable enough first.

Learn more

Fixing bad SMP

Too dark, too blurry, hairline in the wrong spot. A lot of bad SMP can be corrected without laser. Send me photos and I'll be honest about what's fixable.

Learn more
